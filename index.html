We build the world’s highest quality eyewear – precision engineered optical instruments that transcend the characterization as mere fashion accessories.

Providing a product experience more in line with premium Swiss wrist watches than with eyewear as it is commonly perceived, Máttr offers descerning consumers a new dimension of exclusivity and the ability to underline status and sophistication in superior ways as they protect, enhance or correct their vision with the very best modern lens and light filtering technology has to offer…

Individuality

All Máttr optical instruments offer a radical level of customization - from subtly refined to unabashedly extravagant.

Our jewelry-level finish options include precious metal platings and technical coatings, such as platinum, rhodium, and diamond- like-carbon (DLC). In addition, each frame’s entire surface can be engraved in 3D to produce a unique work of art.

For those seeking the zenith of personalization, we offer bespoke designs, precisely tailored from your digital head scan to ensure a perfect fit.
